HOW IT WORKS

1

Pick a stock

Every round, choose one stock from that round's matchups. Never reuse a stock — once it's picked, it's gone for the rest of the season.

2

Survive the round

Whichever stock in a matchup moves more wins. Your pick wins, you're still alive. It loses, you're out.

3

Regular season

32 stocks, split into conferences and divisions, playing a full schedule — just like the format you already know.

4

Playoffs & payout

Survive to the end and it's winner take all — or the remaining survivors can agree to split the pot at any point.
